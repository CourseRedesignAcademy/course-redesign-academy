Designing for Shortened Terms: A Course Redesign Academy

Developed in support of the 16 colleges participating in Achieving the Dream’s Scaling Shortened Academic Terms (SSAT) initiative, this two-week academy provides faculty with practical strategies for intentionally designing, redesigning, and refining courses for seven- and eight-week terms.

Register for an Academy

Who should attend

Designed for faculty preparing to teach in a seven- or eight-week term or seeking to strengthen a course already offered in that format.

  • Faculty preparing to teach in a seven- or eight-week term.
  • Faculty seeking to improve or strengthen an existing shortened-term course.
  • Instructional designers who want to apply the redesign process to a course or instructional design project and complete activities alongside faculty participants.

What you'll gain

A focused redesign

Make informed decisions about learning outcomes, assessments, pacing, learning activities, and student support.

Practical tools and templates

Use guided activities to apply and document your redesign decisions.

Ideas and feedback

Exchange approaches with colleagues during live sessions.

Next steps

Identify priorities for completing your initial redesign and applying the same approach across the rest of your course.

How it works

This two-week online Academy combines self-paced Canvas activities with one 90-minute live session each week. You'll apply the redesign process to one manageable portion of a course and submit assignments that document your progress.

Week 1 | Module 1

Set Your Priorities. Prioritize learning outcomes and examine how well assessments measure what students need to learn. Apply your decisions through guided activities and a live session.

Week 2 | Module 2

Design the Learning Experience. Plan the sequence and pace of learning, practice, and feedback; refine activities; and consider student support. Identify next steps for putting your redesign into practice.
