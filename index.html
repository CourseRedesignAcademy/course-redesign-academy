Who it's for

The academy is built for faculty who are actively teaching or about to teach in a shortened term. Bring one specific unit from a course you teach: the work applies to it across both weeks.

  • Faculty converting an existing unit to an eight-week or other compressed format
  • Faculty already teaching in shortened terms who want to strengthen a specific unit's pacing, workload, and assessment
  • Department chairs and program leads preparing a group of courses for a shortened-term rollout
  • Instructional designers and teaching-and-learning staff supporting faculty through the transition

What you get

Each week produces a real piece of the redesign. By the end you have a finished unit, not a folder of ideas.

One redesigned unit

Outcomes, sequencing, activities, and assessment for one unit — fully mapped and ready to teach in a shortened-term calendar.

A concrete transition plan

The academy ends with a Unit Transition Plan: the specific steps that move this unit from its current form into the compressed version.

Feedback from peers

Structured review of your redesign by faculty from other institutions working through the same constraints in different disciplines.

A record of completion

A letter of completion from Achieving the Dream documenting your participation and the work you produced.

How it works

Each academy is two weeks long, fully online through Canvas with a weekly 90-minute live session.

Week 1 — Module 1

Foundations of Course Redesign. Work through backward design and outcome prioritization at your own pace, then meet live for Session 1 to apply it to your unit. The week ends with your first deliverable: an assessment audit of the unit you're redesigning.

Week 2 — Module 2

Mapping and Redesigning Your Unit. Map your unit day by day, refine activities, and meet live again for Session 2. The academy ends with your finished Unit Transition Plan.
