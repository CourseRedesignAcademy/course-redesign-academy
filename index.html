Redesign your course for a shortened term — with a cohort doing the same work.

A facilitated academy for faculty moving courses into eight-week and other compressed formats. You leave with a redesigned course you can teach next term, not a plan to redesign it someday.

Who it's for

The academy is built for faculty who are actively teaching or about to teach in a shortened term. Bring a specific course — the work is applied to it throughout.

  • Faculty converting an existing 16-week course to an eight-week or compressed format
  • Faculty already teaching in shortened terms who want to strengthen pacing, workload, and assessment
  • Department chairs and program leads preparing a group of courses for a shortened-term rollout
  • Instructional designers and teaching-and-learning staff supporting faculty through the transition

What you get

Every session produces a piece of your redesigned course. By the end you have a full set, not a folder of ideas.

A redesigned course

A revised schedule, assessment plan, and workload map for one specific course, built to fit the compressed calendar you actually teach.

Pacing that holds up

Practical approaches to sequencing, prioritizing essential outcomes, and cutting what a shortened term can't carry — without lowering the bar.

Feedback from peers

Structured review of your redesign by faculty from other institutions working through the same constraints in different disciplines.

A record of completion

A letter of completion from Achieving the Dream documenting your participation and the work you produced.

How it works

Live virtual sessions with a facilitator, supported by a Canvas course space for materials, submissions, and between-session work. Expect two to three hours of independent work between sessions.

Before you start

Choose the course you'll redesign and submit a short baseline description in Canvas.

Sessions 1–2

Identify essential outcomes and rebuild the term schedule around them.

Sessions 3–4

Redesign assessment and workload for the compressed calendar.

Wrap-up

Peer review, revision, and submission of your completed redesign.
